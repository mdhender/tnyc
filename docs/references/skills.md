# Skills

This is the consolidated T'Nyc skill list. It combines the original skills from
the [rules](rules.md) (section 3) with the skills accepted in the
[GURPS 4e skill gap review](../research/gurps-skill-gaps.md), and records the
closest GURPS Fourth Edition skills for each.

The GURPS column is a functional mapping, not a claim of mechanical
equivalence. T'Nyc skills are deliberately broader than GURPS skills.

> **Unverified GURPS names.** The GURPS skill and college names below have not
> yet been checked against the Basic Set. Treat them as provisional until that
> check is done.

## Ratings and costs

Skill ratings run from 1 (knowledge of some basics) to 10 (total mastery).
Skills are learned and improved with STUDY and cost silver to learn and to
maintain (rules 3.4). Magic skills cost far more than other skills.

Skill numbers are grouped by kind, and within a group a higher number usually
costs more. The grouping is a convention inherited from the original rules, not
a design rule.

| Range | Group |
|---|---|
| 1–31 | Personal, combat, and production |
| 32–49 | Magic |
| 50–64 | Provincial production |
| 65+ | Movement |

## New skill descriptions

The original skills are described in rules section 3.3. These descriptions
cover the skills added since.

### 26 Siegecraft

Siegecraft is the art of taking and holding walled places: siege engines,
mining under walls, breaching gates, and the defense of cities, castles,
towers, and fortresses against the same.

### 27 Logistics

Logistics keeps armies supplied with food, pack animals, and freight on the
march and in the field. Well-supplied armies travel farther and suffer less
from attrition.

### 42 Dispelling

Dispelling ends the magic of others: spells, illusions, summoned beings, and
the undead. Where Magic Resistance protects only its holder, Dispelling acts
on magic already at work in the world.

### 43 Beast Mastery

Beast Mastery is the art of capturing, taming, and commanding animals and
magical creatures. Taming a magical creature is one of the ways a character
without Air Magic can fly (rules 3.35).

### 44 Enchantment

Enchantment is the making, identifying, and unmaking of magical artifacts.
Artifacts are THINGs in the game, and Enchantment is how new ones enter the
world.

### 53 Smithing

Smithing covers the working of metal into tools, arms, and armor. Like the
other provincial production skills, it lets a province produce trade goods
(see [issue #6](https://github.com/mdhender/tnyc/issues/6)).

### 66 Horse Riding

Horse Riding lets a character ride, and so move at horse speeds. Rules 3.32.2
says that Cavalry automatically bestows it; the skill had no number until now.
